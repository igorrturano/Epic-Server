# Game Design Document — Weapon Balance

## A. Design pillars (locked)

1. **No meta weapon.** Every viable build has a defensible answer to "which weapon do I want?"
2. **One ladder, family slices.** Same DPS curve `T_n = 5 + n` for all. Each family occupies a slice paid for by its structural cost.
3. **Craft pays off.** Pearson r between craft skill and DPS must be **positive** in every family. CI enforces.
4. **Buff up, don't nerf down.** Players shouldn't see flat damage drops on weapons they already use. The only nerfs are *situational shape changes* (finesse meta gets armorpierce — same DPS vs heavy armor, lower vs unarmored).
5. **Posture is flexibility, not multiplier.** Multi-slot weapons trade dice ceiling for tactical pivot.
6. **Declarative tier.** Every weapon has `Tier N`. CI fails if `|measured DPS - TIER_TARGET[N]| > 0.5`.

## B. The power budget

`Power = DPS + Utility`. Within a tier, weapons trade DPS for utility; `Power` stays flat.

**Utility levers:**
- Range (~+1 tier vs melee equivalent — pays in ammo)
- Shield-slot eligibility (defensive benefit — caps offensive ceiling)
- Posture flexibility (~−0.5 DPS for 2-3 slot — gain tactical pivot)
- Special crit script (armorpierce, bleed, stun — situational)
- Hit-roll skill choice (Wrestling vs Sword/Mace/Fencing — skill cost)
- Dual-wield offhand (~85% extra swings — pays in no-shield + Dual_Wield + halved offhand Tactics)

## F2. Accepted deviations (signed off — not bugs)

Surfaced by independent audit, consciously accepted by the design owner:

- **N2 — dual_wield true premium ≈ +21% (not +25%).** The engine never crits the dual-wield offhand swing ([DamageController.inc:921-924](pkg/systems/combat/include/Controller/DamageController.inc#L921-L924)); the model now matches that (crit EV applies to mainhand only). Net: dual_wield's apex Dex premium is ~21%, ~4pp under the 25-35% band floor, *only* for dual_wield (1H/2H single-swing land ~25-26%). **Accepted** — a ~4pp lower premium on the highest-DPS family is fine; it slightly narrows dual_wield's lead over 1H/2H, which is benign.
- **N3 — collateral engine nerf to ~6-9 un-retiered finesse weapons.** The `Dex/15→/20` engine change reduces the per-hit stat bonus of every finesse weapon. Finesse weapons in wrestling/ranged/throwing (`leque`, `kama`, `nunchaku`, `luvaComGarras`, `lajatang`, `socalLaminado`, and any finesse ranged/throwing) were *not* retiered to absorb it, so they lose ~5-6% effective DPS. **Accepted as collateral** — these families were explicitly out of scope (wrestling de-prioritized, ranged Dex-only by design, throwing P0); a future pass can retier them if it matters.
